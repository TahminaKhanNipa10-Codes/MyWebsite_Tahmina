About Heart to Hand

Our Mission

Heart to Hand is a donation platform dedicated to helping people in need across Bangladesh. We aim to connect donors directly with causes and individuals, ensuring transparency and impact.

Our Impact

Since our launch, Heart to Hand has successfully connected over 10,000 donors to various campaigns. Thousands of people have received aid in education, healthcare, and emergency relief through our platform.

Our goal is to expand further and make giving simple, effective, and meaningful for everyone.
